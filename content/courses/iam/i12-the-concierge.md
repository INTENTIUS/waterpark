## Outcome

`wp-request` (D0) and the reference Environment (D1):
checkout, SKILL.md, default-deny egress naming the verb service and the
code host, no cloud credentials; intake-identity enrollment on the leaf
file; refusals that name the escalation path.

## Steps

1. `chant run wp-request` with typed args produces the PR with no agent.
2. The Environment manifest (chant-lexicon-fountain can declare it);
   `fountain apply` stands up the concierge as a teammate.
3. "payments-api needs read on the invoices bucket" (scenario 6): one-file
   PR, lint and the proof pass, access delta rendered by the deterministic
   renderer, not the model.
4. An unmapped identity: a refusal naming the enrollment route, no PR. A
   boundary exception: a directed refusal to the platform path.
5. Add this app's rows to F4's table (the PR-only token, the gated apply
   role, what the sandbox holds) and fill the parts table from
   [the propose loop](../../propose-loop.md).

## Done when

Prescription 13 and D1's acceptance criteria.

## Solo

Floci plus a Fountain instance; the verb service runs beside it.
On a runner (F10) the egress claim is void; say so.

## Live

Hosted sandbox provider; beat 1 of every playlist is the
Environment manifest on screen. The punchline: the teammate that built the
repo in I1 just used it, and nobody trusted it at any point.
