Twelve lessons that teach [Fountain](https://github.com/BinaryBourbon/fountain) by building up to the [propose loop](../../propose-loop/): abstracted from Mend, Rounds and dns-desk, which every scenario instantiates. F0 is the thesis (an agent is the newest hire). F1–F7 are the runtime: primitives, lifecycle, egress, credentials, the team, schedules, apps. F8 and F9 are the loop, interactive and ambient, with the three apps as worked references. F10 and F11 are the two things to be honest about: your own machine is not a sandbox, and Fountain has no gate in the loop.

Solo needs a Fountain instance (self-hosted is fine), an inference key, and a GitHub repo you own. Live needs a hosted sandbox provider for anything that claims containment.
