## Outcome

You have read the fourteen properties and can say, for the
scenario you are about to run, which ones it leans on and which it skips.

## Steps

1. Read [accessibleops.net](https://accessibleops.net): fourteen properties,
   two sentences each, tagged by what they buy (speed, confidence,
   transparency, repeatability, disposability).
2. Read *The two halves of day two*: synthesis properties (can a stranger
   predict the spec before it runs) and lifecycle properties (does the risky
   change stop for a human, does the reversible one go through).
3. Pick the scenario (IAM, DNS, Mend) and mark its property list from the
   coverage table in [plan.md](../../docs/plan.md).

## Done when

You can name the property each later lesson is about without
looking at the lesson.

## Solo

Ten minutes of reading.

## Live

The facilitator reads the thesis line out loud and asks the room
which property their last incident violated. Two minutes.

[principles.md](../../docs/principles.md) maps the IAM invariants onto
the properties.
