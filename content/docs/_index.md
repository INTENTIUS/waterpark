These are the documents the lessons send you to. The [decisions ledger](decisions/) is the why, written down. The [house rules](principles/) and the [prescriptions](prescriptions/) are the pattern the IAM course builds, made checkable. [splashdown](demo-org/) is the company. The [threat model](threat-model/) is the failure-mode story. [The AWS desk](aws-desk/) is the agent app the courses build. The notes under design/ carry the depth behind single lessons.

The kit-era docs say "water park" where they mean the IAM access-repo kit these courses grew out of. Project planning and archived analysis live outside the site, in [project/](https://github.com/INTENTIUS/waterpark/tree/main/project).
