Sixteen lessons, plus an appendix, that build [pepperoni](../../docs/demo-org/)'s access repo from nothing: one resource type per file, PR-only writes, guardrails failing in the editor, drift caught, delegation bounded by IAM itself. The concierge takes the desk's form (propose is a PR, approval is the merge); the watcher takes Rounds'; Mend runs on the access repo as-is. Each lesson names the Accessible Ops property it teaches and the [prescription](../../docs/prescriptions/) it closes; the [principles](../../docs/principles/) and the [decisions ledger](../../docs/decisions/) are the why.

Solo deploys to Floci and says per lesson what Floci cannot show. Live deploys to real sandbox accounts from checkpoints; the [facilitator guide](../../docs/demo/) has the playlists.
