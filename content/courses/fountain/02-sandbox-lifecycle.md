## Context

- A conversation runs in a sandbox with its own disk.
- An idle sandbox suspends after 60 minutes by default and keeps its disk. A sandbox is destroyed after 24 hours by default and loses its disk. The transcript survives both and the conversation stays resumable.
- Self-hosted instances can change both limits.
