These are the documents the lessons send you to. The [decisions ledger](decisions/) is the why, written down. The [house rules](principles/) and the [prescriptions](prescriptions/) are the pattern the IAM course builds, made checkable. [The estate](estate/) is what the repo manages, which is water park's own AWS. The [threat model](threat-model/) is the failure-mode story. [The AWS desk](aws-desk/) is the agent app the courses build. The notes under design/ carry the depth behind single lessons.

Some of these docs were written when water park was an IAM access-repo kit rather than a course, and a few still read that way. Where they name a toolchain, decision 31 is what the course actually runs on. Project planning and archived analysis live outside the site, in [project/](https://github.com/INTENTIUS/waterpark/tree/main/project).
